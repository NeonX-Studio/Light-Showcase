# The world of Light

## Light City

Light City is an underground civilization built after a surface catastrophe. Most residents have inherited an official history that presents underground life as the natural order of humanity. Access to information is therefore as important as access to food, energy, and physical space.

The city is organized into three broad layers:

- **Information layer:** a small population controlling records, policy, and long-term decisions.
- **Industrial layer:** manufacturing, energy, logistics, and the infrastructure that keeps the city alive.
- **Agricultural layer:** food production, maintenance work, population pressure, and the main starting environment for the player.

These layers are connected by material flows and separated by status, access permissions, and unequal knowledge.

## Player perspective

The player starts as **Extro Martinez**, a maintenance worker whose routine assignments expose the gap between official explanations and the way the city actually functions. The intended experience is built around investigation, negotiation, compromise, and the consequences of choosing who receives information or resources.

## Narrative principles

- The world continues to operate when the player is not present.
- Information is local, incomplete, and shaped by authority.
- NPCs remember meaningful interactions and can revise their attitude over time.
- Resource shortages and political choices create pressure before a quest is explicitly written.
- There is no single moral route that cleanly resolves every conflict.

The title **Light** refers both to the natural surface that most residents have never seen and to the possibility of recovering a more truthful account of the past.
